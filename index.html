How I work with AI

Define the goal

Who it's for and the one thing it has to achieve.

Choose the right tools

Pick the tool that fits the job, from Claude to Lovable or Make, instead of forcing one tool to do everything.

Bring what I know

Start from my own knowledge of business, marketing and the brand, so the AI builds on solid ground.

Prompt with care

Clear, detailed prompts with real context: brand books, CVs, constraints and examples.

Iterate and check

Compare options, refine, and review facts, tone, code and mobile layout myself.

Ship and improve

Publish, gather feedback and keep improving.
